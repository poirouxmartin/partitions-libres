# Batch 1: skeleton of the open scores app (15/11/2026)

Working name: partitions-libres. Provisional display name « Partitions libres », defined in one place (`app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml`, `app_name`). Identifier: `com.musique.partitions`.

## Done

- **Modules**
  - `:catalog`: pure Kotlin. Model, catalogue loading, search, versions and releases.
  - `:render`: reusable Android library. Verovio 6.4.1 over JNI, MusicXML/MXL to SVG then Picture. It depends on neither Compose nor Views, so the composition app can reuse it (see `render/README.md`).
  - `:app`: Compose UI.
- **Catalogue**: 3,725 pieces, generated by `tools/build_catalog.py` into `app/src/main/assets/catalog.json` (2.5 MB, bundled).
  - Sources:
    - OpenScore Lieder: 1,414 pieces, CC0, MusicXML.
    - OpenScore String Quartets: 196 pieces, CC0, MusicXML.
    - Mutopia: 2,115 pieces, PDF engraved with LilyPond.
  - Licences: CC0 1,610, public domain 1,107, CC BY-SA 765, CC BY 243. No NC or ND; the script excludes them, and the Kotlin parser does too.
  - Each piece carries its licence and licence link, the engraver, the source, the source link and the original edition.
- **Search** by composer, title, work and instrument. It ignores accents and accepts transliterations: « Rachmaninov » finds « Rachmaninoff », likewise for Tchaïkovski, Scriabine, Moussorgski, Prokofiev, Dvořák, Schönberg. It tolerates a typo and sorts in natural order (no. 2 before no. 10).
- **Score page** with an Attribution box always visible. For BY-SA, it states: « Fichier diffusé tel quel, sans modification ».
- **Reader**
  - MusicXML: Verovio layout recomputed at screen width.
  - PDF: PdfRenderer, page by page.
  - An attribution banner stays permanently displayed (licence, engraving, source).
- **Offline**: « Télécharger » saves the file as is, with a JSON sheet next to it, which keeps the attribution offline. A « Mes partitions » section appears on the home screen. Verified in airplane mode, cache cleared, cold start.
- **Update**: the app queries the GitHub releases of the `updateRepo` repository, at most once every 12 h, silently on errors. The banner offers « Installer » (APK download, then Android installer) or « Plus tard ».
- **Icon**: adaptive. Cream music page with a staff and a copper eighth note, on an ink-blue background, plus a monochrome version. « Papier et encre » theme, light and dark.
- **« À propos et licences » screen**: catalogue policy, sources, Verovio (LGPL-3.0), androidsvg and AndroidX (Apache-2.0) notices.

## Checks

- Build passes: `gradlew :catalog:test :render:testDebugUnitTest :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleDebug :app:assembleRelease`.
- 41 JVM tests, all green: catalog 25, render 6, app 10.
- Screenshots on emulator (Pixel 8, x86_64) in `docs/captures/`: `recherche-rachmaninov.png` (10 results), `partition-rachmaninov-pdf.png`, `partition-musicxml-verovio.png`, `fiche-piece.png`, `lecture-hors-ligne.png`, `accueil.png`, `icone.png`.
- Bug found and fixed on the emulator: on a cold start, a piece opened directly showed « introuvable » (not found), because the piece lookup was not recomputed once the catalogue had loaded.

## What remains

1. **Badly spaced lyrics** in the MusicXML rendering: words run together (« Lord,forto-morrow »), and two stray dots appear at the top left. Probable cause: the Verovio SVG spaces are not handled by androidsvg. To fix in `:render`.
2. **Slow startup**: the 2.5 MB catalogue takes about 8 s to load on the emulator, in debug. Options: measure in release, precompute the index, load as a stream, or use a binary format.
3. **Hosting**: files are for now taken from the sources (raw.githubusercontent.com for OpenScore, at a frozen commit; mutopiaproject.org). The study recommends hosting them ourselves.
4. **Update** (17/11): checked at startup and on each return to the app (network at most every 6 h, 30 min after a failure, known update kept offline); « Plus tard » postpones by 24 h; APK accepted only from the repository's releases, same package id and higher versionCode. Release APK signed with a stable key kept outside the repository, arm64-v8a + x86_64, 43 MB. Remaining: publish the repository (public) and a first release to try it end to end.
5. **Study page turns**: taps, half page, Bluetooth pedal, automatic cropping. Also remaining: favourites separate from downloads, instrument filter and transposition.
6. **Final name** and visual identity: the icon is provisional but consistent with the theme.
7. **IMSLP**: nothing without a written agreement. However, OpenScore cites the IMSLP reference of the transcribed edition, as simple provenance.
8. **Mutopia composers**: they only have the first initial (« S. Rachmaninoff »), to be enriched (Wikidata).
